Methodology

The adapter projects the leading national teams by total men's World Cup appearances using public historical tables plus the 2026 participating field from FIFA match data.

National team tournament appearance totals run through the 2026 finals: every 2026 participant is credited a tournament, while non-qualifiers such as Italy and Serbia are unchanged from 2022.
