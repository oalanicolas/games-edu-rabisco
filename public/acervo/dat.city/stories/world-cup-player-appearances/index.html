Notes

Methodology

The adapter projects leading men's World Cup player appearance totals from public historical record tables, then adds 2026 appearances from FIFA match data for active players.

Player appearance totals combine historical record tables with 2026 tournament participation. Lionel Messi (34) extends his record after playing all of Argentina's matches to the final; Cristiano Ronaldo reaches 27 after Portugal's run.
