Notes

Methodology

This adapter parses Wikipedia's annual World's Billionaires tables, reads the age and net-worth columns, and selects the youngest distinct people who appear in the annual top-10 snapshots. The latest frame is a bootstrap comparison frame using each selected person's youngest observed table appearance.

Observed Wikipedia's annual Forbes world-billionaires tables through 2026. This is a bootstrap view of the youngest people appearing in those annual top-10 tables, not a complete under-30 Forbes list.
