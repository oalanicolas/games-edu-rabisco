Notes

Methodology

The adapter projects an all-time men's World Cup goals leaderboard from FIFA/Wikipedia historical record tables, then adds current 2026 tournament goals from FIFA's public GameDay scorer feed for active players. A modeled by-date backfill animates the 2026 all-time-record chase across the tournament calendar.

Live men's World Cup goal totals combine through-2022 historical baselines with FIFA's 2026 tournament goal feed. After the 2026 final, Kylian Mbappe (22) overtook Lionel Messi (21) as the all-time leading men's World Cup scorer. The checked-in live snapshot is the fallback when FIFA's public feed is unavailable.
