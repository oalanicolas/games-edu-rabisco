Notes

Methodology

The adapter reconstructs one ranking frame per year from archived Wikipedia captures (checked-in backfill) and appends a freshly fetched current-year frame from the live Wikipedia table. Each frame ranks albums by claimed worldwide sales (millions); rank movement reflects real editorial revisions and list churn over time. Artist portraits are pulled from Wikipedia and packed into the WebP sprite atlas.
