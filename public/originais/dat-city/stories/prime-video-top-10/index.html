Methodology

Wikipedia does not currently provide a durable weekly/current Top 10 page for Prime Video. This bootstrap uses the configured Wikipedia original-programming and original-film list pages as the source universe, then ranks titles by recent en.wikipedia pageviews via Wikipedia's PageViewInfo API. Treat the output as public-interest attention among platform originals, not streaming watch time or official platform ranking.
