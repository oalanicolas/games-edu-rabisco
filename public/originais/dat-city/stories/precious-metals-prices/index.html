Notes

Methodology

This adapter reads the LBMA free JSON price feeds (gold, silver, platinum, palladium), takes the latest published USD auction fix per metal, and converts USD per troy ounce to USD per kilogram. Deep annual history comes from the LBMA backfill; scheduled StoryOps runs append daily fixes so the district replays observed price moves.

Observed the LBMA USD auction fixes through 2026-08-03. Prices are the published USD/ozt fix converted to USD/kg (x32.1507466).
