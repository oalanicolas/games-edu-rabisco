Notes

Methodology

This StoryOps adapter combines Forbes' annual 'world's highest-paid actors' and 'highest-paid actresses' rankings into one earnings leaderboard per year, re-ranking every listed performer by their published Forbes estimate. The yearly sequence is the historical backfill, so the race chart animates real year-over-year pay movement across Hollywood; portraits are enriched from Wikipedia.

Combined Forbes highest-paid actors and actresses earnings for 2013-2020 (8 annual frames). Each figure is Forbes' published pre-tax trailing-12-month estimate in USD; the two gendered lists are merged and re-ranked by earnings. Forbes did not publish comparable lists for every earlier year, so verified coverage begins in 2013.
