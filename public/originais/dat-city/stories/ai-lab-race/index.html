Notes

Methodology

This adapter reads the public Artificial Analysis homepage payload, groups scored models by the lab that created them, and ranks each lab by the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index of its best released model. Historical frames are Internet Archive captures of the same homepage, so past standings reflect what Artificial Analysis actually published at the time. Recent frames replay model release dates, so a lab's tower jumps the day it ships a stronger frontier model.
