How Token Delta works

This is every token served through OpenRouter since January 2025, as one river. Time runs from the spring at the back (the first week of January 2025) to the edge at the front (the last seven days), where the river pours over. Every channel is one model, coloured by the lab that made it, and as wide as the tokens it served that week. Channels of one lab run side by side; the islands between them are land, not data.

The river widens because usage did: about 0.5T tokens a week at the spring, 163.6T at the edge. The wooden plaques name the models that were number one for a while, at the week they took the top. Mist hangs over stealth models: models served under a code name before anyone says whose they are.

What you can do

Play runs the 21 months: the river lights up week by week, and the card reads who led and by how much. The slider picks any week. Width: Tokens draws each channel as wide as its tokens, so the river grows; Share draws it as wide as its share of that week, so the river holds still and you can see the channels trade places. Hover a channel for its numbers, click it to follow it down the river.
