What you are looking at

The timeline says Google's next model will go toe to toe with Fable and Astra. Nobody has a number for it yet, so here it is the way the memes draw it: one enormous golden dragon, and three small ones that have stopped moving.

The honest bit

The big dragon is a rumor. It has no score, so its bar on the board is a question mark. The three small dragons are real: each is that lab's best model today on the Intelligence Index, and right now they are the ones on top. Google's best released model is here too: it is the little sibling cheering behind the big one, and the last row on the board, so you can see how far the rumor would have to jump.

Three scenes

1 is the boss fight, with today's real scores. 2 is the same dragon with three heads, all of them silly. 3 is two heads that mean it and one that does not. Scenes 2 and 3 are posing: nothing in them is a score.

Play with it

The cast is yours. The card at the bottom left lists every tag on stage: tap a lab chip to pick another lab from the airace roster, type any name, and in scenes 2 and 3 pick each head's face. Shuffle the cast deals a random one. Copy link gives you a link that opens exactly your cast, for sharing. It stays in your browser; reset puts the default cast back. The scoreboard shows today's real scores for the default cast only, so it steps aside while yours is on stage.

Click the ground and the dragon puts a bolt there. Both carved stones are links. Space makes it roar and slam. C hands the camera to the director, and your first drag takes it back.
